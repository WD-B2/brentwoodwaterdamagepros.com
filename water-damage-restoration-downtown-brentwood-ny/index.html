If Water Is Still Spreading

Do these things while you wait for the crew, in this order.

Make it safe

Stay out of standing water near outlets, cords or a wet panel. Call your utility or 911 for sparks, a burning smell or a gas odor.

Stop the source

Close the fixture valve or the main shutoff if you can reach it from dry floor. Leave the rest to the crew.

Protect what is dry

Lift small items, electronics and papers onto dry surfaces. Skip household fans and ordinary vacuums on wet floors or contaminated water.

Photograph the damage

Take photos and short video from a safe spot before anything is moved. Check your policy and call your insurer about coverage.

A Short Local Note: The Modern Times Core

The streets around the station sit on the oldest part of the hamlet. Modern Times, a utopian village founded in 1851, was renamed Brentwood in 1864. The Modern Times School, built in 1857, stands at Third Avenue and First Street and joined the National Register of Historic Places in 1994.

What it means for repairs

Some buildings here are old and have been altered many times. When we open a wall, we plan for mixed piping, older wiring and finishes that need testing before they are disturbed.

What we do not claim

We do not claim that older buildings suffer more water damage, only that repair planning should account for what is inside the walls.

What to Do and What Not to Do After a Downtown Leak

Follow these steps while you wait for the crew.

  • Shut the source and tell the property manager or the neighbor above or below.
  • Do not walk through standing water near outlets, a panel or a refrigerator cord.
  • Do not use a household vacuum or fans to clear water from a floor or ceiling.
  • Photograph the damage from a safe spot and list affected stock or belongings.
  • Drying goals follow the ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage guide, which we cite as a benchmark and not as a certification claim.

Downtown Brentwood Notes

Brentwood is an unincorporated hamlet in the Town of Islip, so permits for building and plumbing work go through the Town of Islip Building Division. Ask before a repair that opens walls or changes plumbing.
