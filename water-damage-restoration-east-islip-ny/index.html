Emergency Water Damage in East Islip: What to Do Right Now

Dispatch runs day and night. Same-day response is available when crews are free and conditions are safe. We do not promise a fixed arrival time, but we tell you plainly what we can do when you call.

If Water Is Still Spreading

Do these things while you wait for the crew, in this order.

Stop the source

Close the fixture valve or the main shutoff. If you cannot reach it without wading through water near outlets, stay out and call.

Cut power to wet rooms

Switch off breakers for wet areas from a dry spot. Do not use a vacuum or space heater on wet floors.

Lift and protect

Move small furniture, electronics and papers onto dry surfaces. Foil under furniture legs limits stain transfer on wet carpet.

Photograph the damage

Take photos and short video before anything is moved. New York DFS advises documenting losses before cleaning up.

Before the Next Storm Warning

Small steps before a storm reduce what you have to clean up after it.

  • Test your sump pump if you have one and clear gutters and downspouts.
  • Move stored items off basement floors onto shelves.
  • Know where your main water shutoff and electrical panel are.
  • Keep photos of your home and belongings in a safe place for claims.
  • Save your insurer's claim number so you can file quickly.

Standards We Use for East Islip Jobs

Homes near the South Shore and canals can see storm water that counts as contaminated. The ANSI/IICRC S500 guide classes it as Category 3, so porous materials are usually removed. We cite the guide as a benchmark, not as a certification claim.
