About Brentwood Water Damage Pros

We are a water damage restoration team based in Brentwood, NY. Here is how we work and what you can expect when you call.

Who We Are

Brentwood Water Damage Pros is based at 739 Suffolk Avenue in Brentwood, NY. We restore homes and businesses after water losses, from burst pipes to flooded basements to sewage backups.

We are licensed and insured. We keep our claims simple: we tell you what we do, how we price it and what the standards are, and we do not make promises we cannot keep.

How We Work

  • We answer the phone 24/7 and respond the same day when crews are free.
  • We explain the scope and the price before work starts.
  • We read moisture with meters and a thermal camera and log the readings.
  • We tell you when a problem belongs to another trade.
  • We document the loss for your insurer.

Standards We Work To

The ANSI/IICRC S500 guide is the industry reference for water damage restoration, and the S520 guide is the reference for mold remediation. We use them to describe water categories and drying goals. Naming a standard describes the benchmark we work to. It is not a claim of certification.

Honest Limits

We restore water damage. We do not waterproof foundations, repair roofs or fix plumbing, and we say so when your problem belongs to those trades. In New York, mold assessment and mold remediation on the same project must be done by different licensees, and we explain how that works on the mold page.

We are not medical professionals and do not make health claims. Talk to a physician about symptoms.

What Happens When You Call

Every call is answered by a person at the emergency line, day or night.

We listen first

We ask what happened, where the water is and whether power is on near the water. We tell you what to do while you wait, such as closing a valve or switching off a breaker from a dry spot.

We plan the crew

Based on your answers, we send the right equipment. A burst pipe in a kitchen needs different tools from a flooded basement or a sewage backup.

We explain the scope

On site, we read moisture and explain what is wet and why. We tell you how the price is built before any equipment goes in.

The Tools We Use

Restoration is part science, part equipment.

Extraction

Portable and truck-mounted extraction units remove bulk water from floors, carpet and low areas.

Drying

Air movers push air across wet surfaces and LGR dehumidifiers pull moisture out of the air.

Measurement

Moisture meters and a thermal camera show wet areas and let us log readings at each visit.

Water Categories in Plain Words

Restoration standards describe water by how contaminated it is. Category 1 is clean water from a supply line. Category 2 is gray water, such as from a washing machine or dishwasher drain. Category 3 is black water, such as sewage or floodwater.

The category decides what can be dried and what must be thrown away. Carpet that took clean water can sometimes be saved. Carpet that touched sewage cannot.

Short promises we can keep.

Honest limits

If your problem belongs to a plumber, roofer, waterproofing contractor or licensed mold assessor, we say so.

Who Calls Us

Homeowners call most often, usually after a burst pipe, an appliance failure or a basement that took on water. Landlords and property managers call when a tenant reports a leak. Business owners call when a sprinkler head, roof drain or supply line floods a shop or office.

Many callers are also dealing with an insurer. We keep photographs, readings and a written scope so those conversations go smoothly, and we stay out of coverage decisions, which belong to you and your insurer.
