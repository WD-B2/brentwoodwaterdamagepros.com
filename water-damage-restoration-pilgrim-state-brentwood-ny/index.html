Water Damage in the Pilgrim State Area

Pilgrim State Hospital opened in Brentwood during the Great Depression, and its patient population peaked at nearly 14,000 in 1954, as reported by Long Island Press. The site is now the Pilgrim Psychiatric Center, and large parts of the old grounds have changed hands and changed use.

Brentwood State Park is on part of that land at 375 Crooked Hill Road. It covers 52 acres, with entrances on Crooked Hill Road and Community College Drive, per the Wikipedia article on the park.

If Water Is Still Spreading

Do these things while you wait for the crew, in this order.

Make it safe

Stay out of standing water near outlets, cords or a wet panel. Call your utility or 911 for sparks, a burning smell or a gas odor.

Stop the source

Close the fixture valve or the main shutoff if you can reach it from dry floor. Leave the rest to the crew.

Protect what is dry

Lift small items, electronics and papers onto dry surfaces. Skip household fans and ordinary vacuums on wet floors or contaminated water.

Photograph the damage

Take photos and short video from a safe spot before anything is moved. Check your policy and call your insurer about coverage.

Water Problems in a Mixed Area

The area is not one housing type, so the causes vary.

Older and institutional buildings

Large buildings can hide long pipe runs and drain lines. A leak may travel far from where the stain shows. See commercial water damage restoration.

New construction

Newer units still have burst supply lines, appliance leaks and water heater failures. See appliance leak water damage.

Open land and parking areas

Heavy rain can send runoff toward a doorway or a lower level. See storm water damage cleanup.

What Is Planned on the Old Grounds

Redevelopment of the former hospital land has been planned for years, and the status of each phase can change.

Heartland Town Square

The project is a mixed-use plan on roughly 452 acres, with more than 9,100 housing units and more than four million square feet of office space, according to Long Island Press. The Islip Town Board approved the first phase, and we have not verified what has been built.

What it means for water damage

New units mean new plumbing and appliances, so ordinary leaks and failures will occur. We do not claim a flood history for this area.

What to Do and What Not to Do After Water Enters

Follow these steps while you wait for the crew.

  • Stay out of water that touches outlets, cords or a wet panel.
  • Do not enter a flooded lower level if water is rising or the source is unknown.
  • Do not rely on household fans or an ordinary vacuum for sewage or storm water.
  • Photograph the damage from a safe spot and note affected rooms.
  • Drying and water categories follow the ANSI/IICRC S500 guide, which we cite as a benchmark and not as a certification claim.
