Burst Pipe Emergency: The First Ten Minutes

Find the main shutoff and close it. In most homes it is near the water meter or where the line enters the basement or utility room. If you cannot find it, close the nearest fixture valve and call.

Next, switch off power to wet areas from the panel if you can reach it dry. Then open a faucet at the lowest level to drain remaining pressure from the lines. Once the flow has stopped, call the 24/7 line.

Do This, Skip That

A short checklist for the first hour.

  • Photograph the pipe, the water and the damaged contents before moving anything.
  • Move valuables and electronics to a dry room.
  • Do not walk through standing water near outlets or a panel.
  • Do not run a vacuum or space heater on a wet floor.
  • Keep receipts for emergency repairs, since insurers ask for them.

Where Burst Pipes Hide

Not every burst announces itself with a geyser. Water under a kitchen sink, behind a bathroom wall or above a ceiling can run for hours before anyone sees it.

Under a sink

A supply line or fitting fails and water spreads across the cabinet floor and out onto the kitchen floor. Cabinet bases and toe kicks swell first. Pull everything out of the cabinet so the crew can see the source.

Inside a wall or ceiling

Stains, bubbling paint or a ceiling that sags are signs of a leak above or behind the surface. Do not poke a sagging ceiling. Water pooled behind it can come down all at once, so see ceiling water damage repair.

Under the slab or in the crawl space

A line below the house is harder to spot. A warm floor spot or a water bill that jumps points to slab leak water damage.

Insurance and Burst Pipe Claims

Sudden pipe failures are commonly handled under the water damage part of a homeowners policy, but policies differ. Read yours or ask your agent. New York DFS advises filing a claim as soon as possible and keeping receipts for emergency repairs.

Permanent repairs should wait until the insurer has inspected the loss, according to DFS. We document readings and photos you can hand to your adjuster.

What to Do and What Not to Do After a Pipe Bursts

Follow these steps while you wait for the crew.

  • Shut the main water valve first, then open a low faucet to drain the lines.
  • Switch off power to wet rooms from the panel only if you can reach it without stepping in water.
  • Do not use a heat gun or open flame on a frozen or split pipe.
  • Do not leave wet drywall and insulation in place for days, because they hold moisture against framing.
