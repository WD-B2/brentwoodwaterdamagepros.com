Water Heater Emergency: Shut It Down Safely

Turn off the cold supply valve to the tank. For an electric heater switch off its breaker. For gas, turn the gas control to off or pilot, as the label instructs. Never stand in water to reach an electric unit.

A standard tank holds many gallons, and a failed tank can release all of it. A slow weeping leak at a fitting is less dramatic but can rot a floor and feed mold over weeks.

Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Is the water coming from the tank, the pipes, the relief valve or the drain pan?
  • Is the floor around it wet in more than one room or into a finished space?
  • Are boxes, drywall bottoms or carpet touching the water?
  • Can the supply valve be reached without stepping through water near an outlet?
  • Is there a plumber on the way to drain, repair or replace the unit?

Why Water Heaters Fail

Most tanks fail from age and corrosion. We do not replace tanks, but we do clean up after them.

Tank corrosion

The steel inner tank rusts through and leaks from the bottom or seams. Once a tank leaks, it does not heal, and replacement is a plumber's job.

Fittings and relief valve

A weeping connection or a temperature and pressure relief valve that opens can run water across the floor. These are often repairable, but the water still needs to be dried.

Drain pan overflow

A pan under the heater catches small leaks. A clogged pan drain lets the pan overflow, which is a common hidden cause.

Insurance Notes for Water Heater Losses

Sudden tank failures are commonly covered, while slow leaks over time often are not. Policies differ, so read yours or ask your agent. DFS advises documenting the loss and keeping receipts for emergency work.

Replacement Planning After a Leaking Tank

  • A plumber replaces the tank and checks the relief valve and the drain pan.
  • Consider a drain pan with a line to a floor drain where practical.
  • Ask about shutoff valves that are easy to reach.
  • Keep the old unit's age and model information for your insurer.

What to Tell Us When You Call

Tell us where the heater sits, whether the room is finished and how much water you think came out. Mention if stored items were on the floor and whether the room below is finished.

A basement with a floor drain behaves differently from a closet on an upper floor, and we plan equipment accordingly.

Water Heaters in Brentwood Basements

Brentwood homes often keep the water heater in a basement or utility room next to the furnace. Water from a failed tank spreads across the floor and under wall bottoms before anyone notices.

Replacement of a water heater usually needs a licensed plumber and sometimes a permit. Ask your building department what applies.
