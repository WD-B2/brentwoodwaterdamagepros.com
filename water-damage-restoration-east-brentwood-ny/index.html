If Water Is Still Spreading

Do these things while you wait for the crew, in this order.

Make it safe

Stay out of standing water near outlets, cords or a wet panel. Call your utility or 911 for sparks, a burning smell or a gas odor.

Stop the source

Close the fixture valve or the main shutoff if you can reach it from dry floor. Leave the rest to the crew.

Protect what is dry

Lift small items, electronics and papers onto dry surfaces. Skip household fans and ordinary vacuums on wet floors or contaminated water.

Photograph the damage

Take photos and short video from a safe spot before anything is moved. Check your policy and call your insurer about coverage.

Common Water Problems in East Brentwood Homes

Most losses start inside the house.

Finished basements

Sump pump failures, drain backups and window wells put water into lower levels. See basement water damage cleanup.

Supply lines and heaters

Braided hoses, water heaters and old valves fail without much warning. See burst pipe water damage.

Laundry rooms and kitchens

Washer and dishwasher lines spill onto floors that carry water to the level below. See appliance leak water damage.

What to Do and What Not to Do in a Wet Basement

Follow these steps while you wait for the crew.

  • Stay out of the basement if water covers outlets or reaches the panel.
  • Do not run a shop vacuum or extension cord through standing water.
  • Do not open a basement door fast if deep water is behind it.
  • Photograph the waterline from the stairs and list affected belongings.
  • Floodwater from outside is Category 3 under the ANSI/IICRC S500 guide, so porous materials are usually removed. We cite it as a benchmark and not as a certification claim.
