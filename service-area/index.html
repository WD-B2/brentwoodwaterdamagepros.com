Seasons and Water Damage Around Brentwood

Winter brings freezing nights, which split pipes in unheated garages and exterior walls. Spring and summer bring heavy rain and thunderstorms, which push water into basements and through roofs. Humid summers also make AC condensate lines and damp basements a common source of trouble.

Each season has its own pattern, but the first steps are always the same: stop the source if it is safe, stay clear of electrical hazards, document the damage and call.

About Suffolk County

These towns are in Suffolk County, where drinking water comes from underground aquifers, according to the Suffolk County Water Authority. Many homes use septic systems and others connect to sewers, so ask whether your address is sewered if you have a drain backup.

For permits, ask your building department. Brentwood, Islip Terrace and East Islip are in the Town of Islip, and Hauppauge sits partly in Islip and partly in Smithtown.
