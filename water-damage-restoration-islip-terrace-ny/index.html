If Water Is Still Spreading

Do these things while you wait for the crew, in this order.

Stop the source

Close the fixture valve or the main shutoff. If you cannot reach it without wading through water near outlets, stay out and call.

Cut power to wet rooms

Switch off breakers for wet areas from a dry spot. Do not use a vacuum or space heater on wet floors.

Lift and protect

Move small furniture, electronics and papers onto dry surfaces. Foil under furniture legs limits stain transfer on wet carpet.

Photograph the damage

Take photos and short video before anything is moved. New York DFS advises documenting losses before cleaning up.

Common Water Problems in Islip Terrace Homes

Islip Terrace is a residential hamlet of single-family houses. The water problems we hear about follow the age and layout of those houses.

Basements that take on water

Finished basements are common in this part of Suffolk County. A failed sump pump, a drain backup or heavy rain can leave an inch of water that wicks into paneling and drywall within hours. See basement water damage cleanup.

Plumbing that has aged

Older supply lines and water heaters fail without much warning. A slow leak can run behind a wall for weeks before a stain shows. See burst pipe water damage.

Laundry rooms and kitchens

Washer hoses and dishwasher lines are among the most frequent sources of leaks in the house, and water tends to find the floor below.
