Ceiling Leak Emergency: What To Do First

A ceiling that is dripping, bulging or stained brown has water above it. Drywall that holds a pool of water weighs a lot and can fail all at once. Do not stand under it and do not poke at it with anything.

Move furniture and rugs aside, set a bucket under steady drips and turn off power to light fixtures in the wet area. Then call. We tell you how to find the source above while the crew is on the way.

Where Ceiling Water Comes From

The stain is below the problem, not on it. We trace the water upward before repairs so the same ceiling is not wet again next month.

Plumbing above

A leaking supply line, drain, toilet seal or tub overflow on the floor above sends water through the subfloor and into the ceiling. Callers often tell us the leak began upstairs in a bathroom or closet and showed up as a wet spot downstairs.

Roof or attic

Missing shingles, flashing gaps or ice dams let water into the attic and onto the ceiling insulation. A roofer fixes the roof. We handle the wet drywall and insulation.

HVAC condensate

A clogged AC drain line can overflow into a ceiling or closet. See AC leak water damage for that case.

How We Repair a Water-Damaged Ceiling

Release and extract

Trapped water is let out in a controlled way, and the wet area is extracted.

Open and dry

Soaked drywall and insulation are removed and the cavity is dried with air movers.

Check for mold

Surfaces and framing are read for moisture so growth does not start behind new drywall.

Rebuild

New drywall, texture and paint match the room once readings are dry.

Ceiling Repair and Mold

A ceiling that stays wet for several days can grow mold on the back of the drywall. If you see dark spots at a corner or smell mustiness, read mold remediation. Fix the leak first, then clean up.

Water stains left unrepaired can also hide growth. A moisture reading tells us whether the stain is old and dry or still wet.

Insulation and Light Fixtures Above a Wet Ceiling

What sits above the drywall changes the repair.

Insulation

Wet fiberglass or cellulose loses its value and holds water against the drywall. It is removed and replaced after the cavity is dry.

Light fixtures

Water can collect in a recessed light housing. Turn off the circuit and keep the fixture off until it is checked and dry.

Ceiling Water Damage and the Industry Standard

Wet ceiling drywall is judged by how long it stayed wet and by where the water came from. The ANSI/IICRC S500 guide groups water into three categories, and that grouping decides whether the board is dried or cut out. Clean supply water for a short time is treated differently from a drain or roof leak that has soaked insulation for days.

Ceiling Leaks in Brentwood Homes

Many Brentwood houses are two-story homes with bathrooms above living areas, so an upstairs plumbing leak often shows up as a ceiling stain below. Asking your landlord, association or plumber about the source is the next step.

If your ceiling work involves opening framing or electrical, ask the building department whether a permit applies. Town of Islip has a Building Division for properties under its jurisdiction.
