Privacy Policy

This page explains what information we collect when you visit this site or call our line.

What We Collect

This website does not have forms or accounts. If you call us, we collect the information you give on the call, such as your name, address, phone number and the details of the water loss. If you email us, we receive your email address and message.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, for security and performance.

Call Recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How We Use Information

We use call and email information to respond to your request, plan work, communicate with you about your job and keep records. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Content

Pages on this site embed Google Maps and load fonts from Google Fonts. Those services may collect technical information such as your IP address under their own policies.

Your Choices

To ask about information we hold about you, email contact@brentwoodwaterdamagepros.com. We will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page.
