If Water Is Still Spreading

Do these things while you wait for the crew, in this order.

Stop the source

Close the fixture valve or the main shutoff. If you cannot reach it without wading through water near outlets, stay out and call.

Cut power to wet rooms

Switch off breakers for wet areas from a dry spot. Do not use a vacuum or space heater on wet floors.

Lift and protect

Move small furniture, electronics and papers onto dry surfaces. Foil under furniture legs limits stain transfer on wet carpet.

Photograph the damage

Take photos and short video before anything is moved. New York DFS advises documenting losses before cleaning up.

Two Towns, Two Building Departments

Part of Hauppauge is in the Town of Islip and part is in the Town of Smithtown. Permits depend on which town your property sits in, so ask the right building department before repairs that open walls or change plumbing.

How to tell

Your tax bill or property record shows which town you are in. Ask your building department if you are unsure.

When a permit may be needed

Structural, electrical and plumbing changes commonly need one. Surface repairs often do not. The building department decides.

What We Document for Insurers After a Hauppauge Loss

Commercial and residential claims both depend on records. We keep them as the job runs.

Moisture logs

Readings by room and material, recorded at each visit, show how drying progressed and when it finished.

Photographs

Before, during and after photos show the water line, the removed materials and the finished repair.

A written scope

A plain description of what was wet, what was removed and what was repaired gives your adjuster something concrete to review.

Standards We Use for Hauppauge Jobs
